What a strategy observation means

Repeated purchases, sector exposure, and concentration can be studied from public transactions, but they don't tell us an investor's motive. India uses only named NSE bulk/block deal records; quarterly shareholding reports may offer a different, delayed perspective. Canada uses verified early-warning and corporate investment announcements, which can include private placements. A missing trade is not proof that an investor made no purchases.
