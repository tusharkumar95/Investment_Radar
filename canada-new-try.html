How to interpret this experiment

Verified purchases are reviewed against source documents, with named buyers, quantities, consideration and trade dates. Corporate private placements are labelled differently from open-market purchases. Joint actors count as one independent buyer group; repeated purchases are not confused with total ownership. Research queue headlines are NOT buy signals. U.S. 13D/13G/13F notices represent filings, not confirmed same-day transactions or purchases of Canadian CDRs. SEDI and SEDAR+ access is manual or otherwise authorized; they are not scraped.

Source status will appear here. Missing prices are not filled with estimated values.
