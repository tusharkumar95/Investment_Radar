How to interpret this experiment

A confirmed acquisition is a documented ownership transaction, but filing dates can follow trade dates. A price/volume alert is only a market-behaviour observation; it does not identify a buyer. CDRs trade in CAD and generally offer notional hedging, but they are not identical to U.S. shares. Hedging costs, spreads and depositary ratios affect returns. Signals are research leads, not buy recommendations.
