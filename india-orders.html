Source reliability

The poller checks official NSE and BSE RSS feeds during Indian market hours. Exchange feed availability varies; it may run late or return incomplete summaries. Only an original source reviewed for contract value earns the “Source-verified” label. A feed notice with a rupee amount remains “Attachment needs review” until checked. What qualifies as a large order?

For now, the alert floor is ₹100 crore of awarded contract value (or an issuer's documented order-size classification with a lower bound of at least ₹100 crore). The default filters can be adjusted above. Revenue significance cannot be estimated without verified annual turnover, and a ₹100 crore order can be huge for one business but immaterial to another. Orders may span multiple years, may contain cancellation clauses, and can be modified.

This is a research alert, not a buy recommendation. Publicly reported award values can differ from ultimate executed revenue. The exchange source PDF should always be reviewed for scope, duration and customer.
